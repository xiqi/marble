# About Marble

Marble is building software that helps restaurant teams understand and act on their operations. The work starts with the records restaurants already have: purchases, recipes, sales, counts, and the context that lives in people's heads and messages.

A useful system connects those sources, remembers what matters, and helps an operator decide what deserves attention. That might mean explaining a change in ingredient usage, spotting something worth checking before an order, or recognizing that a confident answer would require information the restaurant has not captured.

This assignment is a small expression of that direction. We want to see how you turn operational evidence into useful judgment, and how your design deals with changing or unreliable information. You can choose the product surface and technical approach. This is a description of the exercise and product direction, not a specification of Marble's existing capabilities.

All restaurant records in this packet are synthetic. We do not use candidate submissions as production work product.
